## First Time Login

### Receiving Your Credentials

You should have received an email with:
- 🌐 **Dashboard URL**: https://clinic.healthcompanion.com
- 📧 **Your Email**: Your registered email address
- 🔑 **Temporary Password**: A randomly generated password

### Logging In

1. Open the dashboard URL in your web browser
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter the temporary password from your email
4. Click **"Log In"**

### Important First Steps

After logging in with your temporary password, you will be **automatically redirected** to the Change Password page. You cannot access other features until you change your password.

---

## Changing Your Password

### Why Change Password?

For security, all new clinic accounts require a password change on first login. Your temporary password was:
- Generated automatically
- Sent via email (less secure)
- May be known to system administrators

### How to Change

1. On the Change Password page, enter:
   - **Current Password**: Your temporary password
   - **New Password**: Your chosen secure password (minimum 8 characters)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your new password

2. Password Requirements:
   - ✅ Minimum 8 characters
   - ✅ Mix of letters and numbers recommended
   - ✅ Avoid common words or patterns

3. Click **"Change Password"**

4. ✅ Success! You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Password Tips

**Good Passwords**:
- HealthClinic2024!
- MyCl!n1cPass
- SecureAccess#99

**Avoid**:
- password123
- Your clinic name
- Your date of birth

### Changing Password Later

To change your password after first login:
1. Click your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **"Change Password"**
3. Follow the same steps

### Forgot Password?

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password"**
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Follow instructions in the email
5. Set a new password

## Managing Your Profile

### Accessing Profile

1. Click **"Profile"** in the left sidebar
2. Or click **"Edit Profile"** on the dashboard

### Editable Fields

**Clinic Information**:
- Clinic Name
- Phone Number
- Street Address
- City
- District/State
- Country

**Location Coordinates**:
- Latitude (-90 to 90)
- Longitude (-180 to 180)

**Operating Hours** (optional):
- Set opening and closing times for each day
- Leave blank for days you're closed
- Use 24-hour format (09:00, 17:00, etc.)

### Updating Your Profile

1. Click in any field to edit
2. Make your changes
3. Click **"Save Changes"** at the bottom
4. ✅ You'll see a success message

### Finding Your Coordinates

If you don't know your exact coordinates:

1. Go to [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com)
2. Search for your clinic address
3. Right-click on your location marker
4. Click on the coordinates that appear
5. They'll be copied to your clipboard
6. Paste into the Latitude and Longitude fields

**Format**:
- First number = Latitude (like 40.7128)
- Second number = Longitude (like -74.0060)

### Why Coordinates Matter

Accurate coordinates ensure:
- ✅ Patients can find you on the map
- ✅ Distance calculations are correct
- ✅ Your clinic appears for nearby patients
- ✅ Navigation directions work properly

### Operating Hours

**Setting Hours**:
- Select day of the week
- Pick opening time (e.g., 09:00)
- Pick closing time (e.g., 17:00)
- Repeat for each day

**Tips**:
- Set realistic hours (when you actually accept patients)
- Update if your hours change seasonally
- Mark lunch breaks as closed hours if needed

**Example Schedule**:
```
Monday:    09:00 - 17:00
Tuesday:   09:00 - 17:00  
Wednesday: 09:00 - 17:00
Thursday:  09:00 - 17:00
Friday:    09:00 - 16:00
Saturday:  10:00 - 14:00
Sunday:    Closed
```

## Managing Specialties

### Accessing Specialties

1. Click **"Specialties"** in the left sidebar
2. Or click **"Manage Specialties"** on the dashboard

### Available Specialties

Select all that apply to your clinic:

- ☐ **General Medicine**: General practice, family medicine
- ☐ **Cardiology**: Heart and cardiovascular conditions
- ☐ **Dermatology**: Skin conditions and diseases
- ☐ **Endocrinology**: Hormones, diabetes, thyroid
- ☐ **Gastroenterology**: Digestive system, stomach, intestines
- ☐ **Neurology**: Brain, nerves, neurological conditions
- ☐ **Oncology**: Cancer diagnosis and treatment
- ☐ **Orthopedics**: Bones, joints, muscles, ligaments
- ☐ **Pediatrics**: Children's healthcare
- ☐ **Psychiatry**: Mental health and psychiatric conditions
- ☐ **Pulmonology**: Lungs, respiratory conditions

### Selecting Specialties

1. Check the box next to each specialty you offer
2. You must select **at least one specialty**
3. Select all that your clinic genuinely provides
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Why Specialties Matter

Your selected specialties determine:
- 🎯 Which patients see your clinic
- 🔍 When your clinic appears in search results
- 📊 What types of cases you handle

**Example**:
- Patient diagnosed with diabetes → Endocrinology clinics recommended
- Patient with recurring asthma → Pulmonology clinics recommended
- Patient with skin rash → Dermatology clinics recommended

### Best Practices

✅ **DO**:
- Select all specialties you actually provide
- Include General Medicine if you're a multi-specialty clinic
- Update specialties when you add new services

❌ **DON'T**:
- Select specialties you don't offer (damages trust)
- Leave specialties unchecked if you provide them (missed opportunities)
- Forget to save after making changes

## How Recommendations Work

### When Patients See Your Clinic

Your clinic appears in patient recommendations when:

1. **Pattern-Based Recommendations**:
   - Patient has **recurring disease** (3+ occurrences in 90 days)
   - Patient has **persistent disease** (active > 30 days)
   - Patient has **chronic condition** (matches their history)

2. **Fallback Recommendations**:
   - No nearby pharmacies have the prescribed medicine
   - System suggests clinics as alternative care option

### Matching Process

The system matches patients to your clinic based on:
1. **Disease-Specialty Mapping**: Patient's diagnosis → Your specialties
2. **Distance**: Within 100km of patient's location
3. **Active Status**: Your clinic is active
4. **Operating Hours**: Shown if you're currently open

### What Patients See

When your clinic is recommended, patients see:
- 📍 Clinic name and address
- 🏥 Medical specialties you offer
- 📏 Distance from their location
- 📞 Your phone number
- 🕐 Whether you're open now
- 🗺️ Map and navigation buttons

### Recommendation Reasons

Patients see different messages based on why you're recommended:

**"Recurring Disease Detected"**
- "Your diagnosis history shows a recurring pattern"
- Orange notice with occurrence count

**"Persistent Disease"**
- "This condition has persisted for an extended period"
- Amber notice with duration

**"Chronic Condition Match"**
- "Your symptoms match a chronic condition"
- Red notice with condition name

**"Specialist Care Recommended"**
- "No nearby pharmacies found with medication"
- Blue notice (fallback mode)

### Patient Actions

Patients can:
- 📞 **Call**: Directly call your clinic's phone number
- 🗺️ **Navigate**: Open GPS navigation to your location
- ℹ️ **View Details**: See full clinic information
- 🔍 **Filter**: Search by specialty

## Troubleshooting

### I Can't Log In

**Problem**: Login fails with "Invalid credentials"

**Solutions**:
1. Check email is typed correctly (no spaces)
2. Ensure password is correct (case-sensitive)
3. If first login, use temporary password from email
4. Try "Forgot Password" if you've changed it before
5. Contact admin if still unable to access

### My Profile Won't Save

**Problem**: Error message when saving changes

**Check**:
- All required fields are filled (*)
- Phone number is in valid format
- Coordinates are valid numbers (-90 to 90, -180 to 180)
- Internet connection is stable

### Patients Aren't Finding My Clinic

**Problem**: Low or no recommendations

**Possible Reasons**:
1. **Profile Incomplete**: Complete all required fields
2. **No Specialties**: Add at least one medical specialty
3. **Wrong Coordinates**: Verify location is accurate
4. **Clinic Deactivated**: Contact admin to check status
5. **Outside Service Area**: Patients must be within 100km

**To Verify**:
- Check dashboard shows "Active" status
- Confirm at least one specialty is selected
- Test coordinates on Google Maps
- Ask admin to check your clinic in their system

### Coordinates Are Wrong

**Problem**: Clinic appears in wrong location

**Solution**:
1. Go to Google Maps
2. Find your actual clinic location
3. Right-click and copy coordinates
4. Update in Profile → Location
5. Save changes
6. Verify on map display

## FAQs

### How often should I update my profile?

Update your profile whenever:
- Contact information changes
- You add new medical specialties
- Operating hours change (seasonal, holidays)
- You move to a new location
- Phone number changes

### Can I temporarily close my clinic?

Contact the system administrator to:
- Deactivate your clinic (for renovations, vacations)
- Reactivate when ready to accept patients again

While deactivated:
- Patients won't see your clinic in recommendations
- Your profile remains in the system
- You can still log in and update information

### How are distances calculated?

- Uses your clinic's latitude and longitude coordinates
- Calculates straight-line distance to patient's location
- Shows results within 100km radius
- Sorted by distance (nearest first)

### What if I offer a specialty not listed?

Contact the administrator to:
- Request addition of new specialties
- Suggest updates to the specialty list

Current specialties cover most common medical fields.

### How do I know if patients are finding me?

Check your dashboard for:
- **Recommendations** count: Number of patients who've seen your clinic
- **Analytics** section: Detailed statistics (coming soon)

### Can patients book appointments through this system?

Currently, patients can:
- ✅ See your clinic information
- ✅ Call your phone number directly
- ✅ Navigate to your location
- ❌ Book appointments (not yet available)

They must call you to schedule appointments.

### What happens if I forget to change my password?

For security:
- You'll be locked to the Change Password page
- Cannot access other features until changed
- If you lose temporary password, use "Forgot Password"

### How do I log out?

1. Click your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **"Logout"**
3. You'll be returned to the login page

Always log out on shared computers!
